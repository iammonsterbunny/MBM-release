## ✦ Why MBM?

MBM is built around one idea: your music should play the way you want, without accounts, paywalls, or interruptions.

| | |
|---|---|
| 🔓 **No account** | Open the app and start listening. Nothing to sign up for. |
| 💸 **No subscription** | There is no paid tier standing between you and your music. |
| 🚫 **No ads in the app** | Your listening stays uninterrupted. |
| 🗂️ **Your library, your control** | Playlists, Favorites, and history stay local or on the server you choose. |

## ✦ Features

### 🔍 Search & discover
- Search a broad catalog of **songs, videos, and albums**, with suggestions as you type.
- **Paste a music or video link** to play it instantly.
- Browse by **genre**, and discover artists, popular tracks, albums, and singles.
- **List search**: enter comma-separated song names, review or replace the matches, then play, favorite, or add them to a playlist.

### 🎧 Playback
- Open an album and play it in its **original order** or **shuffle** it.
- **Save albums as playlists** in one step.
- **Background playback** with system media, lock-screen, and headphone controls on supported platforms.
- Switch from audio to **music videos** where available, with standard and HD quality options.
- **Cinema mode** for full-screen video, plus a **floating mini video player**.

### 📝 Lyrics
- **Synced lyrics** that follow the line you're hearing.
- **Seek by tapping a timed line.**
- Plain lyrics and instrumental-track support.
- *Lyrics aren't available for every song.*

### 📜 Queue & radio
- **Play next**, reorder your queue, and manage it freely.
- **Shuffle**, **repeat one**, and **repeat queue**.
- **Autoplay radio** that keeps going with similar tracks.

### 📚 Library
- **Playlists**, **Favorites**, and **Recently Played**.
- Remove individual history entries, or clear history without touching your playlists or Favorites.
- **Export a versioned backup**, preview it, then **merge** or **replace** when you import it on another installation.
- *Export a backup before clearing app data or uninstalling.*

### 🎨 Personalization
- Dark and light appearance, accent colors, and adjustable interface layout and panels.
- Multiple language options and playback preferences.

### 📱 Android home-screen widgets
- Compact, mini, and expanded widget sizes.
- Playback controls, artwork, optional lyrics, and appearance settings.
- *Widgets are available on Android only.*

## ✦ How it works

1. **Search or paste.** MBM looks up the catalog and prepares an available audio or video stream.
2. **Stream, don't store.** Audio is streamed rather than saved as a permanent library file. HD video may be cached temporarily.
3. **Pick your setup.**
   - **Desktop / self-hosted:** the MBM server you choose handles catalog and stream requests.
   - **Android / iPhone:** requests run from the phone itself. No separate computer or server is needed.
4. **Your library stays yours.** Playlists, Favorites, history, and preferences are stored locally, or in the data location of the server you chose.

> An internet connection is required to search and play online content. Availability can vary by track, region, network, platform, or changes made by content providers.

## ✦ Privacy

- **No account required.** Library data is stored in the app's private storage, or in the data directory of the self-hosted server you choose.
- **Network requests are required** for search, playback, and updates. Your IP address is visible to the content and update services involved.
- **No MBM analytics or advertising trackers** are included in the app source.
- **Sharing** uses your operating system's share sheet, or copies a public track link where supported.
- **You control your data.** Remove items from Recently Played, Favorites, and playlists at any time. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local data, so export a backup first.
- If you run your own server, its operator may access server logs and library files.
